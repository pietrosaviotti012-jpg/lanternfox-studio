# Strange Days, part 1 — The Dark Day of 1780

Voce: Kokoro am_michael 1.12 · 38,7 s di voce, video 39,3 s · 120 parole

What if the sky went black in the middle of the morning?
It happened in New England, on May nineteenth, seventeen eighty.
By noon, people lit candles.
Chickens went to roost. Birds went silent.
People thought it was the end of the world.
In Connecticut, lawmakers wanted to adjourn.
But Abraham Davenport refused. If it was Judgment Day, he said, he'd rather be found doing his duty.
He asked for candles, and the work went on.
The real cause? Huge wildfires in the forests of Canada.
Their smoke drifted south, mixed with fog, and blotted out the sun.
Old tree rings still carry the scars of those fires.
What would you have done? Tell me in the comments. Strange Days, part one.

## Fonti dei fatti
- 19 maggio 1780, New England: buio a metà mattina, candele accese a mezzogiorno, polli al posatoio, uccelli in silenzio, molti temono il Giudizio (resoconti dell'epoca).
- Abraham Davenport, Consiglio del Connecticut (Hartford): rifiuta di aggiornare la seduta ("if it is, I choose to be found doing my duty... let candles be brought", riferito da Timothy Dwight).
- Causa: fumo di incendi boschivi in Canada (Ontario) unito a nebbia e nuvole basse; cicatrici da incendio negli anelli degli alberi datate 1780 (studio University of Missouri, 2008).
